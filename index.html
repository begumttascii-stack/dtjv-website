About Us

Gemeinsam • Aktiv • Umwelt • Verbunden

Deutsch-Türkischer Jugendverein e.V. is a youth organization that has been carrying out activities under the name Interkulturelle Organisationen since 2018 and today continues its operations as an official association.

Our core mission is to strengthen understanding, communication, and solidarity among different cultures, and to support the personal development and social participation of youth. Through non-formal education, workshops, and various youth activities, we provide young people with new skills and experiences.

While supporting cultural diversity, tolerance, and integration, we combat discrimination and xenophobia, and encourage the active participation of individuals from diverse cultural backgrounds in society. Through Erasmus+ and international youth projects, we bring together young people from different countries, strengthening intercultural learning and cooperation.

Bringing different cultures together, empowering youth, and working for a more inclusive future.

We Focus On

Our Main Priorities & Goals

Youth Empowerment

Equipping young people with essential life skills, non-formal education tools, and leadership qualities.

Sustainability & Environment

Promoting sustainability, eco-friendly habits, and nature protection projects (Umwelt) across generations.

Active Living & Sports

Encouraging physical health, sports activities, and energetic community participation among youth.

Digital Skills & Media Literacy

Enhancing technological awareness, digital education, and safe online media usage for modern youth.

Intercultural Dialogue

Bridging communities and fostering deep mutual understanding between German, Turkish, and international youth.

Youth Mobility

Creating international exchanges, travels, and cooperative projects through European programs like Erasmus+.
